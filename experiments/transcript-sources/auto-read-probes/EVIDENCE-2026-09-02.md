## Stage 4 route decision

Probe A remains disqualified, while the corrected fixed Active route satisfies
the technical transport gate:

1. Probe A retrieved a real non-empty body and restored the original CC state,
   but it caused a user-visible caption flash. That is an explicit hard stop.
2. Probe B succeeded on the historical English control and both required
   Chinese/English targets with one fixed IOS player request and one json3
   timedtext request. The earlier failures were caused by the probe timer bug
   before network dispatch.

The fixed IOS/json3 Active route is the sole automatic product candidate. It
may enter the product only after cache and Passive miss, may never switch
client or format, and must keep the manual YouTube CC path as the first UNKNOWN
fallback. Page-selected Chinese variants are equal, manual beats ASR, equal
types retain YouTube order, and no-Chinese videos use the current track or the
page default. Panel remains experiment-only.

Product integration still requires offline gates plus a fresh CC-off visual
acceptance proving that the side panel reads both target videos without a
caption flash or translation request for the Chinese source.
